# Skill: Cover Letter Generation

**Purpose:** Generate concise, evidence-based cover letters for target job postings.

## Structure:
1. **Why Company & Role:** Explicit connection between posting requirements and candidate background.
2. **Relevant Evidence:** Specific achievements (Coca-Cola D2C governance via B2Bstack, Avalia Solar production SaaS architecture).
3. **Value Delivered:** How candidate skills (DevOps, SRE, Azure, Observability, CI/CD) solve company challenges.
4. **Professional Closing:** Polite call to action.

## Rules:
- Avoid generic filler phrases ("I am passionate about...").
- Rely strictly on verified facts.
