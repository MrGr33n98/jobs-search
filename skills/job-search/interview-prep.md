# Skill: Interview Briefing & Prep

**Purpose:** Generate a complete interview preparation dossier when a job status transitions to `interviewing`.

## Outputs:
1. **Company Summary & Business Model**
2. **Technical Topic Matrix** (DevOps, SRE, Azure, NRQL, Docker, CI/CD, Rails)
3. **STAR Method Stories** (B2Bstack Coca-Cola D2C pipeline governance, MTTR reduction via n8n/Power Automate, Avalia Solar zero-downtime containerized infra)
4. **Targeted Questions for Candidate to Ask Interviewer**
